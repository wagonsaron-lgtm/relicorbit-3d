Relics of Exploration

Humanity's greatest adventures didn't all return home. These machines remain — silent witnesses to our first steps into the cosmos.

Moon 1969

Apollo 11 LM Descent Stage

The descent stage of the Eagle lunar module that carried Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin to the lunar surface. Left behind at Tranquility Base to reduce ascent weight.

Deployed: July 20, 1969
0.674°N, 23.473°E — Mare Tranquillitatis
NASA.gov
Mars 2012

Curiosity Rover

A car-sized robotic explorer that revolutionized our understanding of Mars. Still operational after a planned 2-year mission, now roaming Gale Crater for over a decade.

Landed: August 6, 2012
4.589°S, 137.442°E — Gale Crater
NASA.gov
Mars 1976

Viking 1 Lander

The first U.S. spacecraft to successfully land on Mars. Operated for over 6 years — far beyond its 90-day design life — conducting humanity's first on-site search for life.

Landed: July 20, 1976
22.27°N, 49.97°W — Chryse Planitia
NASA.gov
